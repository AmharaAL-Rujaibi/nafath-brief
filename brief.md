## Overview
A government entity plans to introduce an AI chat assistant in its mobile application to help citizens find accurate information about public services. Software engineering supports reliable development, version control, and safe updates as the assistant evolves. Artificial intelligence helps interpret questions and retrieve relevant information from approved government resources. Mobile technology provides an accessible interface and protects communication between the application and its services. Networking connects users, servers, and information systems so requests and responses can travel reliably. Cybersecurity is essential to defend the assistant against malicious instructions and unauthorized access. Data management supports responsible handling of citizen information, including privacy and appropriate use of records. These six fields must work together to deliver a useful, trustworthy, and maintainable public service.
